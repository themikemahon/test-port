SMS Terms

Mahonmade LLC · Effective October 30, 2026

These terms cover text messages sent by Mahonmade LLC, a creative studio based in Maplewood, New Jersey.

Program: Mahonmade Weekly Digest

The Mahonmade Weekly Digest is a short text sent on Sunday mornings that previews the week ahead at Mahonmade: project deadlines, scheduled client calls and blocks of focused work. It contains no marketing or promotional content.

How to opt in

You can opt in by texting START to the Mahonmade number, or by giving written consent directly to Mike Mahon, owner of Mahonmade LLC, after being told what the message contains and how often it is sent. Opting in is never a condition of any purchase or service.

Message frequency

About one message per week, sent on Sundays. Occasionally a short follow-up may be sent if the week's plans change.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You'll get one confirmation text and no further messages. Reply START to subscribe again.

Help

Reply HELP to any message for help, or email mike@mahonmade.com.

Carriers and delivery

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Delivery depends on your carrier and device.

Privacy

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy for details.
