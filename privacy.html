Privacy Policy

Mahonmade LLC · Effective October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information Mahonmade LLC ("Mahonmade," "we," "us") collects through mahonmade.com and our text messaging program, how we use it, and the choices you have. Mahonmade LLC is a creative studio based in Maplewood, New Jersey.

Information we collect

  • Contact form details. If you reach out through the contact form, we receive your name, email address and the message you send.
  • Phone numbers and text consent. If you opt in to receive text messages from Mahonmade, we keep your mobile number, the date and method of your opt-in, and any replies you send (such as STOP or HELP).
  • Basic site analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand general traffic to the site, such as pages visited and approximate location. This data is aggregated and is not used to identify you.

How we use it

  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To send the text messages you opted in to, and to honor opt-out and help requests.
  • To understand and improve how the site is used.

Text messaging and mobile information

No mobile information will be sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We use a messaging provider to deliver texts on our behalf. That provider processes your number only to deliver our messages and handle replies. Details on the messaging program, including message frequency and how to opt out, are in our SMS Terms.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the site and our messaging (for example, hosting, analytics, email delivery and text delivery), and only as needed for them to provide those services, or when required by law.

Retention

We keep contact form messages as long as needed to respond and keep a record of our correspondence. We keep phone numbers and consent records while you are subscribed to texts and for a reasonable period after you opt out, so we can show that your opt-out was honored.

Your choices

  • Reply STOP to any text to stop receiving messages.
  • Email us to ask what information we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date.
